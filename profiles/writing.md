# Writing Profile

Use for reports, papers, documentation, proposals, and other source-grounded writing.

## Required Behavior

- Identify the authoritative source version and preserve prior drafts.
- Distinguish sourced facts, user-provided claims, interpretation, and unresolved placeholders.
- Never invent citations, quotations, reviewer comments, policies, results, or approvals.
- Keep terminology consistent and replace internal shorthand when the intended reader would not understand it.
- For exact quotations or marked revisions, preserve the source wording and location.
- Record which sections changed and what checks were performed.
- Formatting-only work must not silently change meaning.
- Publication, submission, or external distribution requires separate explicit authorization.

Do not require a version-comparison memo, full citation audit, or style conversion unless the prompt requests it.
