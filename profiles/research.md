# Research Profile

Use for experiments, simulations, quantitative analysis, literature-backed claims, and research manuscripts.

## Required Behavior

- Identify data, code, configuration, model/checkpoint, and environment sources relevant to the task.
- Never invent observations, citations, measurements, statistical significance, or experimental completion.
- Separate exploratory findings from formal evidence.
- Preserve raw inputs and prior results; write new runs to distinct locations.
- Record enough effective configuration and commands to understand or reproduce the task at its actual risk level.
- Validate scientific success from outputs and semantics, not process completion alone.
- Treat negative findings as valid results.
- Escalate a contradiction in scientific assumptions, metrics, or interpretation to Reviewer/user discussion.

## Proportionality

A small exploratory check does not require a publication package, exhaustive provenance audit, independent reimplementation, or broad literature search. Formal claims require stronger evidence, but that requirement must be named in the approved prompt.
